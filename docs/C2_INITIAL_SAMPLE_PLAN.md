# Phase C2 initial sample plan

Record these selection rules before candidate retrieval. The goal is a three-case packet for practicing independent source annotation on real public abstracts. This convenience sample does not qualify for acceptance scoring.

Use these searches in order, restricted to the named publisher domains. Consider up to the first three results per search that point to individual publisher articles. Save those URLs in ranked order as the replayable candidate frame. Search ranking can change, so use the saved frame for replay.

| Slot | Search query | Domain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| C2-INIT-01 | PLOS ONE spatial navigation virtual environment experiment 2024 2025 abstract | journals.plos.org |
| C2-INIT-02 | Frontiers in Education retrieval practice classroom students study 2024 2025 abstract | frontiersin.org |
| C2-INIT-03 | Astronomy Astrophysics exoplanet transit observational results 2024 2025 abstract | aanda.org |

For each slot, take the first eligible result after checking the saved candidate order. Require an original empirical research article published in 2024 or 2025, relevant to the slot, with an English abstract readable at the publisher without an account. Confirm the DOI, title, year and abstract on the publisher page. Exclude reviews, editorials, corrections, protocols without findings, inaccessible abstracts and duplicate works. Compare normalized DOI, normalized title and case IDs against earlier evaluation metadata and already selected cases. Read only identity fields from earlier manifests, including any pending holdouts. Record an exclusion reason for each rejected entry considered before selection. If no candidate qualifies, record the slot as empty.

Distribute publisher references with verified article-specific licence where available. Use references alone where rights are unclear. For source annotation, the coordinator captures only each publisher's labeled abstract, records capture date and SHA-256, and supplies stable span locators before either reviewer starts. Citations and metadata in this packet use the publisher links. The three slots cover selected topics, years and publisher sites. They do not establish coverage of other topics, languages, abstract styles or robustness cases.
