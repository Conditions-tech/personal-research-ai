In every case, the context record DOI also matches its saved provider source-observation ID. The response fields match the decoded saved response. This verifies record binding in these artifacts; it does not authenticate the underlying publication or validate its scientific claims.

The generated response carries excerpts and a DOI record key, while title, authors, year and source URL remain in the captured paper context. There are no page/section locations or claim-specific bibliographic citations. Exact excerpt presence shows where a string came from, but does not establish that it supports the assigned role, entails the summary, or is scientifically correct.

## Acceptance implications and next actions

The prior developer review's clear background-as-dataset and ecological-result-as-limitation failures remain material. This pass also shows why the application's exact-quote gate is necessary but insufficient: a response can be wholly composed of verbatim sentences and still assign a result to dataset/limitations, duplicate fields, or omit a second major finding. Usefulness varies considerably by paper. The identity checks passed, but they establish traceability to the captured record only; they do not establish correct scientific support.

Current live readiness remains below the unchanged 5.5 GiB gate, so preserve the five unattempted cases and let the existing supervisor wait for qualifying memory. If a later controller run is eligible, execute only its single original uncharged case and reconcile that saved response before any further action; do not tune against pending holdout labels. After the already-approved bounded pilot is complete, the remaining quality task is a predeclared, broader and independently adjudicated evaluation of field relevance, omitted information, wrong-role fills, missingness, numerical fidelity and citation support. No model/app promotion, Phase C completion, synthesis, or full-text claim follows from this review.
