# Phase C2 paper analysis acceptance rubric

2026-11-16. This rubric proposes thresholds for a future evaluation of **human-reviewed, abstract-based drafts**. Freeze the evaluation plan and thresholds before generating or revealing candidate outputs. The existing Qwen3 pilot keeps its original protocol, labels and scoring. Use its published audits for the current decision.

## Prepare the evaluation

1. Freeze a fresh holdout of public abstracts, their provenance and source hashes, the intended use, candidate and baseline model digests, prompt versions, run settings and case handling. Use paired cases for comparisons. Keep development cases and the current pilot outside the acceptance holdout. Include multiple publishers, disciplines and abstract styles, with empirical studies, simulations and theory, explicit and absent limitations, qualified or null results, missing abstracts and instruction-shaped inputs. Document the mix and scope of any language claim.
2. Have two reviewers with relevant subject expertise independently label the source before seeing outputs. For each of the six fields record whether the abstract provides support, acceptable evidence spans, and the distinct facts essential to a short summary. Give each essential fact an ID, role and span. Mark genuine ambiguity explicitly. Use a third reviewer to resolve disagreements. Preserve the original judgments, agreement and resolution. Review outputs with model identity masked and order randomized where practical. Record any unmasking.
3. Define the fields consistently. **Objective** is the study's question or aim. **Method** is what the authors did. **Dataset** is the identified sample, corpus, materials or studied inputs. A generic population, outcome or simulation procedure alone supplies no dataset. **Results** are findings of this study, including negative results and qualifications. **Limitations** are constraints on the study or its inference. **Conclusion** is the authors' takeaway or recommendation. A compound sentence can serve two roles when its clauses explicitly support both. An empty field means the applicable evidence was unavailable in the captured abstract.

## Score the outputs

| Measure | Rule and denominator |
| --- | --- |
| Paper identity and literal traceability | Check run ID, record key, evidence hash, DOI or source locator, and retained model/prompt details per returned draft. For each cited excerpt, verify the exact span occurs in that paper's saved abstract and belongs to the cited record. Report matching excerpts out of all cited excerpts, and passing drafts out of all returned drafts. A filled claim with no excerpt fails binding. |
| Field relevance and wrong-role fills | Judge each populated field against its assigned role. Count relevant fields and wrong-role fills out of populated fields, both overall and by role. Background or an unrelated finding placed in a role counts as wrong-role even when quoted exactly. Record ambiguous alternatives with reasons. Gold string disagreement alone cannot decide relevance. |
| Missingness | On returned drafts, count correct blanks and false fills out of fields with no abstract support. Count blank omissions out of fields with abstract support. A wrong quote in an answerable field is a wrong-role or unsupported fill and can leave an essential fact uncovered. |
| Coverage of essential facts | Count predeclared essential fact IDs faithfully represented **in their assigned field** out of all essential fact IDs across eligible assigned papers, including papers with failed or absent drafts. Give no credit for placing a fact in a different role or repeating it. Report separately on completed drafts so interpretation can distinguish generation failure from partial summaries. |
| Claim-level support | Split every filled statement or interpretation into atomic assertions. For each, mark supported, contradicted, unsupported or unresolved against its cited evidence in abstract context. Check attribution, polarity, uncertainty, comparisons, units and numbers. Copying a full sentence can still be misleading when a neighboring sentence supplies a necessary qualification. Assess interpretations under their explicit label. Report counts out of all atomic assertions, keeping unresolved claims visible and outside supported credit. |
| Workflow and robustness | Report assigned, attempted, returned, valid, refused, failed and unattempted cases separately. Distinguish expected refusal with verified cause from a timeout or resource block. On mixed benign/instruction inputs check both withheld or rejected content and coverage of legitimate facts. Wrong-paper and malformed-input rejection checks need explicit challenge cases. Grant correct-blank credit only for a valid returned draft. |

Retain a review worksheet keyed by case, model and field with excerpt location, role verdict, covered fact IDs, per-claim verdicts and reasons. Record material errors per paper as well as aggregate rates. Report raw numerators and denominators, per-role and case-type counts, disagreements and uncertainty intervals that account for grouping by paper. Use “not evaluated” for a zero denominator.

## Proposed go/no-go checklist

The following are proposed minimums for a supervised trial. Agree on the sample size and operating limits before running it. A starting design is at least 60 eligible abstracts with at least 10 adjudicated present and 10 absent instances per field, plus separate robustness and failure cases. Expand recruitment where the denominators fall short. Set the uncertainty method in advance and limit conclusions to the sampled conditions. These targets are project proposals and have no validation data of their own.

| Check | Required for a go under this proposal | Current evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evidence binding | Every returned draft has correct identity and literal traceability. Every wrong-paper or integrity challenge fails closed and preserves reviewer work. | Binding verified on seven saved Qwen3 drafts. Isolated app tests cover wrong-paper and malformed inputs. |
| Material errors | Zero material wrong-role fills, contradictions or unsupported claims in the acceptance sample. Treat a change to study interpretation, such as assigning a result as a limitation or reversing a finding, as material. Resolve uncertain material judgments before deciding. | **No-go.** Saved Qwen3 drafts include material role substitutions. Earlier local app evaluation also returned an injected cure claim. |
| Usefulness | At least 90% eligible draft completion, role relevance, correct blanks and essential fact coverage, each scored separately. At least 95% of atomic assertions supported. For each field, report role relevance, correct blanks and coverage separately and require at least 80% on each evaluated rate. | **Unverified.** Frozen audits score field selections. They lack a predeclared fact inventory, atomic claim judgments and independent reviewers. Zero empty-field omissions in returned pilot drafts does not capture missed findings inside filled fields. |
| Robustness and operations | Pass the predeclared challenge cases, retain original notes through failures, preserve human approval and reopen saved work. Demonstrate completion under intended memory and latency limits. | Isolated tests cover failure preservation and review reopening. Qwen3 robustness and repeated operation at the intended limits remain open. Five frozen cases were still pending at the November 16 baseline. |
| Review quality | Complete independent adjudication on the fresh holdout and report paired baseline results using the same rubric. | Existing labels and addendum are developer assessments. No independent holdout evaluation has been completed. |

Any failed requirement gives a **no-go**. Incomplete or inconclusive checks leave acceptance pending. A go under this proposal would support only a separately reviewed decision about supervised abstract drafts. It would confer no automatic promotion.
